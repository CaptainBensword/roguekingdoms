Map count: 4 home ports · 4 outposts · 3 monster lairs · 8 ordinary spaces. All tiles are arranged as a connected pointy-hex grid.
MOVEMENT ON THE BOARD

Follow the edges.

A March order moves up to two different army groups by one adjacent hex each. A group cannot move twice in a turn. Groups cannot move through occupied hexes. An enemy-held hex must be challenged before your group can occupy it.

Home ports cannot be occupied by rival armies. Monster lairs are challenged from adjacent hexes; the monster remains at its lair while combat is resolved.
