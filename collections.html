CARD COLLECTIONS

Two decks.
Different jobs.

Rogue Kingdoms uses a blue XP deck for scoring and a separate red Skill Card deck for tactical effects. They are not interchangeable.

BLUE DECK / PHYSICAL SET

XP Reward Cards

Sixteen cards. Four copies each of 10, 20, 35, and 50 XP. Defeat a monster, reveal a reward, and add its value to your final score.
