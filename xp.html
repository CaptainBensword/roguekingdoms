Cards.

HOW XP IS EARNED

Monsters pay the reward.

Defeating a monster earns one card from the shared XP deck. Reveal it, add the printed number to your XP total, and keep the card face up.

Outposts and defeated rival armies reward Skill Cards instead. They do not draw from the blue XP deck.
