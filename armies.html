FIELD GUIDE / ARMY GROUPS

Know your
army.

Army groups are the pieces you move across the Shattered Isles. Each group occupies one hex and tracks health as a single shared total.

Shared health is the rule.Individual miniatures do not track separate health. Add damage to the group's shared HP. When it reaches zero, remove the entire group.

HOW ARMY GROUPS WORK

One group. One hex.

All three types use the same movement and normal attack rules. Their health pools are the main difference in the base rules.

Movement

A group moves one hex with a March order. It cannot move twice during a turn or pass through an occupied hex.

Attacks

A group can make one normal attack per turn. Roll one D10 and apply the result as damage.

Target limit

A group can normally be targeted by one normal attack during each player's turn. Skill Cards may override this.

Defeat

At zero HP, remove the whole group from the board. It no longer occupies or controls its hex.

The recommended playtest supply is two Swordsmen groups, two Archer groups, and one Giant group per kingdom. Confirm the final miniature count before producing the physical set.
