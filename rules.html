01

Introduction

Rogue Kingdoms is a territory-control strategy game for two to four players. Each player commands one of four kingdoms across the Shattered Isles. Build your army, challenge neutral monsters, contest outposts, and finish with the strongest score.

This is the first-edition working ruleset. Where the original project brief did not set a number, the site marks the chosen starting value as a playtest decision.

02

Components

  • One 19-hex Shattered Isles board.
  • Four colored captain markers and matching army supplies.
  • Swordsmen groups (four miniatures per group), Archer groups (three miniatures per group), and Giant groups (one miniature per group).
  • Three monster pieces: Reef Beast, Iron Troll, and Storm Drake.
  • Four outpost markers or banners.
  • One ten-sided die (D10).
  • One 16-card blue XP deck: four each of 10, 20, 35, and 50 XP.
  • A separate red Skill Card deck. Its physical card set is still in development.
03

Game setup

  1. Place the 19 hexes in the Shattered Isles layout: four home ports, four neutral outposts, three monster lairs, and eight ordinary neutral hexes.
  2. Each player chooses Crimson/Red, Blue, Gold/Yellow, or Emerald/Green and takes the matching captain and army pieces.
  3. Place the captain marker at its home port. For a simple first test, each kingdom starts with one Swordsmen group on its port and one Archer group on an adjacent empty hex.
  4. Place each monster at its marked lair. Leave all four outposts neutral.
  5. Shuffle the 16 blue XP cards and place them face down. Shuffle the red Skill Card deck separately.
  6. Choose the first player at random. Play proceeds clockwise.
Supply recommendation for testing: use two Swordsmen groups, two Archer groups, and one Giant group per kingdom. The brief defines miniatures per group, but not the number of groups in each player's supply; treat these quantities as a starting component recommendation, not a final production count.
04

The board

The Shattered Isles contains 19 connected hexes:

  • 4 home ports: one for each kingdom. Rival armies cannot occupy a home port.
  • 4 outposts: neutral locations that can be claimed and contested.
  • 3 monster lairs: home to the Reef Beast, Iron Troll, and Storm Drake.
  • 8 ordinary hexes: open spaces that can be held for two points each at the end of the game.

Two hexes are adjacent when they share an edge. One army group occupies one hex. Army groups cannot stack. Monsters remain at their lairs and are challenged from an adjacent hex.

05

The four kingdoms

Players choose Crimson (Red), Blue, Gold (Yellow), or Emerald (Green). Each kingdom has a matching captain, army pieces, Skill Card hand, and XP collection. Kingdoms use identical rules; color identifies control on the board.

Track one shared HP total for each group; do not track individual miniatures. All groups use the same base movement and normal attack procedure. When a group's HP reaches zero, remove the entire group from the board.

07

Movement

A March is one order. Move up to two different army groups by one connected hex each. A group can move at most once during your turn, even if you take two March orders.

  • A group moves only to an adjacent hex.
  • A group cannot move through an occupied hex.
  • A group cannot enter an enemy-held hex until the defending group has been defeated.
  • A group cannot enter a rival home port.
  • A group cannot share a hex with another army group.

When an enemy group is defeated, the attacker may move into the vacated hex as part of that Attack order if the space is legal and empty.

08

Turn structure

At the beginning of your turn, draw Skill Cards until you hold seven, or until the Skill deck runs out. Then take two orders. Resolve each order completely before starting the next.

1Refill handDraw until you hold seven Skill Cards.
2First orderMarch, Attack, Muster, or Claim.
3Second orderChoose another legal order.
4End turnResolve effects and pass clockwise.

Available orders

  • March: Move up to two different army groups one adjacent hex each.
  • Attack: One eligible army group attacks one adjacent rival group or monster.
  • Muster: Recruit one available group from your supply at your home port, provided that the port hex is empty.
  • Claim: Capture an unclaimed or rival-held outpost occupied by one of your army groups. A claim rewards two Skill Cards when eligible.

You may repeat an order if you have a legal target and remaining eligible groups.

09

Combat and the D10

Normal combat uses one ten-sided die (D10). A normal attack is one damage roll.

  1. Choose one of your army groups that has not made a normal attack during your turn.
  2. Choose one enemy army group or monster in an adjacent hex.
  3. Roll the D10 once. Apply the result as damage to the target's shared HP.
  4. If an army group reaches zero HP, remove it. If a rival group was defeated, the attacker may move into its vacated hex if the space is legal.
  5. If the target is a monster and it survives, it immediately counterattacks the attacking group: roll one D10 and apply that damage to the group's shared HP.

One normal attack means one damage roll. Additional damage rolls require a Skill Card or special ability that explicitly grants them. An army group can normally make one attack per turn and can normally be targeted by one normal attack during each player's turn. A Skill Card may override either restriction if its text says so.

D10 PRACTICE ROLL—Roll once to see a damage result.
10

Monsters

Monsters are neutral enemies at the three central lairs. Attack from an adjacent hex. If the monster survives the attack, it counterattacks the attacking group with one D10 damage roll.

11

Outposts

An outpost begins neutral. To capture it, move one of your army groups onto its hex if the hex is empty, then spend one Claim order and place your banner there. A group must occupy the outpost hex to claim it.

If an enemy group holds the outpost, defeat that group first. The victorious group may enter the empty hex, then use a Claim order to take control. Replace the previous player's banner.

  • Capturing an outpost rewards two Skill Cards.
  • To prevent repeated card farming, each player earns the two-card reward only the first time they capture each specific outpost during a game. Reclaiming that same outpost later does not grant another reward.
  • Each outpost you control at the end is worth ten points.
12

Skill Cards

Skill Cards form the red tactical deck. At the beginning of your turn, draw until you hold seven cards. Your hand limit is seven.

Capture an outpost or defeat a rival army group to earn two Skill Cards. If that reward takes you above seven cards, discard down to seven after drawing.

Skill Cards may grant extra attacks, rerolls, defensive bonuses, movement bonuses, healing, or another clearly described tactical advantage. Each physical card must state its target, timing, effect, and duration. A card that changes an attack limit must say so explicitly.

13

XP cards

The blue XP deck has exactly sixteen physical cards: four 10 XP, four 20 XP, four 35 XP, and four 50 XP.

Defeating a monster earns one XP card. Reveal it and keep it face up with your collection. Add all earned XP during final scoring. Outposts and defeated rival armies reward Skill Cards instead; they do not draw from the XP deck.

Victory and scoring

The game ends when the XP deck runs out or after eight complete rounds, whichever happens first. If the deck runs out during a round, finish that round so every active player gets the same number of turns. After round eight, score at the end of that round.

Scoring sourcePoints
Earned XPPrinted XP value
Each controlled outpost10
Each controlled ordinary hex2
Surviving captain10

An ordinary hex is controlled by the player whose army group occupies it at the end. Outposts are controlled by the player whose banner is on them. Home ports and monster lairs do not score as ordinary territory.

The player with the highest total wins. Ties are shared victories. The standard rules do not eliminate players; each captain still in play receives the 10-point captain bonus.

15

Examples of play

Example: a March order

You choose March as your first order. Move one Swordsmen group one connected hex toward Northwatch, and one Archer group one connected hex toward the Reef Beast lair. Neither group moves again this turn.

Example: attacking a rival

Your Swordsmen group is next to a rival Archer group. You spend an Attack order and roll a 7. The rival group loses 7 shared HP. If a later attack reduces it to zero, remove that group; the winning attacker may move into its empty hex if legal.

Example: challenging a monster

Your group attacks the Reef Beast from an adjacent hex and rolls 8 damage. It now has 22 HP. Because it survived, it rolls a D10 counterattack against your group's shared HP. When the Reef Beast is eventually defeated, the player who lands the final blow draws one XP card.

Example: final score

A player finishes with 65 XP, controls two outposts, and occupies three ordinary hexes. Their total is 65 + 20 + 6 + 10 for a surviving captain = 101 points.
