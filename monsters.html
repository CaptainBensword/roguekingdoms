FIELD GUIDE / MONSTERS

Monster
bestiary.

Three monster lairs sit in the middle of the map. Challenge a monster from an adjacent hex, survive its counterattack, and earn one XP card if you defeat it.

Monster HP values are first-edition playtest choices.The game brief establishes the three monsters and their D10 counterattack system, but not their starting HP. These values are proposed for testing and should be adjusted after a few games.
LAIR I
COASTAL HUNTER

Reef Beast

It moves between broken reefs and shallow channels. The Reef Beast has the lowest starting HP of the three lair guardians.

60HP*D10COUNTERATTACK1XP CARD

MONSTER COMBAT

Attack. Then brace for the reply.

  1. Choose a group.Your group must be in a hex adjacent to the monster's lair and must not have used its normal attack this turn.
  2. Roll one D10.Apply the roll as damage to the monster's HP. Skill Cards may change the roll or add damage.
  3. Monster counterattacks.If the monster survives, it rolls one D10 and applies the damage to the attacking group's shared HP.
  4. Claim the reward.When monster HP reaches zero, draw one XP card and keep it face up. The monster returns at full health at the beginning of the next round.
