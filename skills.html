CARD COLLECTION / RED DECK

Skill Cards.

Skill Cards change what an army group can do. They are earned from outposts and rival victories, then held until the timing is right.

HOW THE DECK WORKS

One hand. Seven cards.

The Skill deck is red and separate from the blue XP rewards. At the beginning of your turn, draw until you hold seven cards. Use a card when its timing allows. If you earn cards while already holding seven, discard down to the hand limit after the reward.

  • Capture an outpost: draw two Skill Cards.
  • Defeat a rival army group: draw two Skill Cards.
  • Maximum hand size: seven.
  • Cards that modify normal rules must state exactly which restriction they change.
ABILITY FAMILIES

What a Skill Card can do.

These are ability categories for the future deck, not finished or printable cards.

↗

Movement

Move an army group farther or adjust a movement restriction.

⚔

Extra attacks

Allow an additional damage roll or override the one-attack limit.

⟳

Rerolls

Change a D10 result when a card's timing condition is met.

⬟

Defence

Reduce or prevent damage to a targeted group.

✚

Healing

Restore HP to an eligible army group without exceeding its starting HP.

✦

Tactics

Provide a situational advantage with a clear target and duration.
