GAME OVERVIEW

How Rogue
Kingdoms works.

Command a kingdom across a shared island chain. Your armies fight for space, Skill Cards change the odds, and monster rewards add XP to your final score.

2–4 players2 orders per turn8 rounds maximum
THE OBJECTIVE

Win on more than one front.

Rogue Kingdoms is a territory-control game. You need XP, but XP is only part of the score. Outposts are worth ten points each, ordinary territory is worth two, and a surviving captain adds ten.

Defeating monsters is the main way to earn XP. Capturing outposts and defeating rival army groups earns Skill Cards, which help protect territory or make a decisive attack.

THE TURN

Two orders. Make them count.

At the beginning of your turn, draw Skill Cards until you have seven. Then take two orders. March, Attack, Muster, and Claim are the core actions.
